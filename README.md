🌌 About the Project

For more than half a century, humanity has sent remarkable machines beyond Earth. Rovers have explored alien landscapes, landers have touched distant surfaces, scientific instruments have uncovered hidden secrets, and spacecraft have travelled into the depths of space.

When their missions end, many of these machines remain where their journeys took them.

Their stories, however, are far from over.

Ghosts of Space is an interactive 3D educational game developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, addressing the challenge:

"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."

Players take on the role of a Space Archaeologist, exploring extraterrestrial environments to discover, investigate, and document historical space exploration artifacts.

Accompanied by LUNA, an intelligent and curious robotic companion, players uncover the engineering behind these machines, the missions they supported, and the scientific discoveries they made possible.

From Apollo hardware left on the Moon to robotic explorers that transformed our understanding of Mars, every artifact has a story to tell.

Our goal is to transform historical mission records and scientific information into an accessible, immersive experience that makes space exploration exciting for school-age learners and curious space enthusiasts.



🎯 Our Mission and Objectives

Our aim is to make the history of space exploration more engaging, accessible, and memorable by bringing forgotten space hardware back into the spotlight.

Through interactive exploration and storytelling, Ghosts of Space aims to:

Preserve the stories: Introduce audiences to NASA's retired, abandoned, and inactive exploration hardware.
Make science accessible: Explain the engineering, technology, and scientific principles behind real space missions in an understandable way.
Encourage exploration: Allow players to discover extraterrestrial environments and investigate historical artifacts.
Create emotional connections: Use a companion-driven narrative to help players connect with the machines and the people behind their missions.
Inspire future explorers: Encourage curiosity about astronomy, robotics, planetary science, and space engineering.



👩‍🚀 Target Audience

Ghosts of Space is designed primarily for school-age space enthusiasts and casual learners, while remaining enjoyable for anyone interested in space exploration, science, and technology.

🎮 Game Overview

Ghosts of Space places players in the role of a Space Archaeologist working to locate, investigate, and preserve humanity's historic space exploration artifacts.

The player is accompanied by LUNA, a robotic companion who assists with navigation, artifact detection, scientific investigations, and storytelling.

The expedition is divided into three levels, each introducing a different aspect of space exploration.


🌕 Level 1 — The Silent Moon

Our journey begins on the Moon, the closest celestial body to Earth.

Players explore the lunar surface and discover the remains of historic Apollo missions and scientific experiments.

Exploration highlights:

Discover Apollo landing-site hardware.
Investigate lunar roving vehicles and scientific instruments.
Explore lunar highlands, plains, and crater fields.
Learn about the engineering behind human lunar exploration.
Understand how Apollo missions expanded our knowledge of the Moon.


🔴 Level 2 — The Red Frontier

The expedition continues to Mars, where players uncover the stories of robotic explorers that investigated the Red Planet.

Exploration highlights:

Discover historic Mars rovers and landers.
Explore the Martian landscape.
Investigate robotic exploration technology.
Learn about geological discoveries and evidence of ancient water.
Understand how scientists study the possibility of past habitable environments.


🌌 Level 3 — Beyond the Known

The final expedition takes players beyond planetary surfaces into deep-space exploration.

This is the most challenging level, focusing on spacecraft that explored asteroids, dwarf planets, and distant regions of the Solar System.

Exploration highlights:

Investigate historic deep-space spacecraft.
Recover scientific records and mission telemetry.
Explore spacecraft trajectories and orbital histories.
Learn about asteroid exploration and planetary science.
Discover how humanity continues to explore beyond Earth.

Deep-space artifacts are investigated according to their actual locations and operational status. Orbiting and travelling spacecraft are represented through appropriate signal, telemetry, and mission-data investigations rather than being portrayed as objects on planetary surfaces.



🛰️ Historical Space Artifacts

The initial game prototype features nine selected artifacts across three exploration levels.


🌕 The Moon

1. Apollo 11 Eagle Descent Stage

The descent stage of the Lunar Module that delivered Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin to the lunar surface during humanity's first crewed Moon landing.

Players discover the engineering behind the landing system and learn about the historic Apollo 11 mission.

2. Apollo 12 Intrepid and ALSEP

Explore the Apollo 12 Lunar Module and the Apollo Lunar Surface Experiments Package.

Discover how astronauts deployed scientific instruments to investigate the Moon's environment and interior.

3. Apollo 15 Falcon and Lunar Roving Vehicle

Discover the Lunar Roving Vehicle that allowed astronauts to travel farther across the lunar surface.

Learn about lunar mobility, geological exploration, and the importance of the Apollo 15 mission.


🔴 Mars

4. Viking 1 Lander

Discover one of the first successful American spacecraft to operate on the Martian surface.

Learn about its atmospheric investigations, soil experiments, and search for signs of life.

5. Mars Pathfinder and Sojourner Rover

Meet Sojourner, the first successful rover to operate on Mars.

Explore its innovative mobility system and discover how it helped establish the foundation for future Mars rover missions.

6. Mars Exploration Rover — Spirit

Investigate Spirit's exploration of Gusev Crater and its discoveries about the geological history of Mars.

Learn how a rover designed for a short mission continued exploring for years.


🌌 Deep Space

7. NEAR Shoemaker

Discover the spacecraft that became the first to orbit an asteroid and later landed on the surface of 433 Eros.

Learn about asteroid composition, navigation, and planetary science.

8. Dawn Spacecraft

Explore the spacecraft that studied two major bodies in the asteroid belt: Vesta and Ceres.

Discover how ion propulsion enabled an extraordinary journey between two different celestial bodies.

9. Pioneer 10

Follow the journey of one of humanity's earliest spacecraft to explore the outer Solar System.

Learn about its historic Jupiter encounter and its continued journey outward from the Sun.



🤖 Meet LUNA — Your Robotic Companion

LUNA is an intelligent robotic companion who accompanies the player throughout the expedition.

She is more than a navigation assistant. She is the player's guide, research partner, and connection to the stories behind the artifacts.

Key Capabilities
Intelligent Following: Smoothly follows the astronaut using spring-damper movement mechanics.
Artifact Detection: Identifies nearby points of interest using directional sonar and proximity indicators.
Interactive Assistance: Provides hints and guidance during exploration and investigation.
Contextual Dialogues: Reacts to discoveries, surroundings, and mission progress.
Expressive Personality: Uses different facial expressions and a friendly, curious, occasionally humorous personality.
Educational Storytelling: Shares historical facts, engineering explanations, and interesting stories during exploration and recharge periods.

Through LUNA, the player develops a connection with the expedition that goes beyond simply collecting artifacts.



🔬 Interactive Artifact Investigation

Every artifact offers an opportunity to explore the technology and history behind it.

Features include:

Artifact Scanning: Approach historical equipment and initiate an investigation.
Component Inspection: Examine important subsystems, instruments, antennas, propulsion systems, and mechanical components.
Historical Images: Compare in-game artifacts with real historical photographs.
Technical Diagrams: Explore diagrams that explain the structure and functioning of the equipment.
Educational Challenges: Complete interactive questions and challenges based on the artifact's mission and scientific contributions.
Digital Archive: Preserve discoveries and investigation records for future reference.

The Preview Probe feature provides access to historical photographs and technical diagrams, helping players connect the 3D experience with real-world space hardware.



📝 Mission Reports and Earth Communication

Players act as field researchers, documenting discoveries and transmitting findings to Earth.

The reporting system allows players to:

Compile investigation findings into structured expedition reports.
Review artifact information and scientific discoveries.
Explore engineering, scientific significance, and historical context.
Transmit completed reports to Earth Command.
Earn investigation points and progress through archaeological ranks.

The reporting system reinforces the idea that exploration is not complete until discoveries are documented and shared.



🏠 Research Cabin and Recharge System

After exploration, players return to the habitat to recharge and review their discoveries.

Features include:

Interactive habitat and airlock transitions.
Resource management for astronaut energy, suit integrity, and LUNA's battery.
Recharge and mission-day progression.
Post-investigation debriefs.
Personal conversations and educational storytelling with LUNA.

Recharge periods are also storytelling moments.

LUNA shares the history of the artifact, interesting mission facts, and the challenges faced by the original engineers and scientists.



🎬 Cinematic Storytelling

The experience begins with a cinematic journey through space before transitioning into an interactive mission.

The expedition follows these stages:

Opening Sequence: A cinematic spacecraft journey introduces the player to the mission.
Mission Briefing: LUNA wakes the player and introduces the expedition.
Planetary Exploration: The player lands and begins investigating the assigned environment.
Artifact Discovery: LUNA detects nearby artifacts and guides the player towards them.
Investigation: Players scan, inspect, and document the discovered equipment.
Recharge and Debrief: LUNA shares stories and interesting facts about the discoveries.
Mission Progression: Completed objectives unlock further exploration.
Mission Conclusion: Players report their findings to Earth Command and celebrate their achievements.

The narrative is designed to make players feel like part of a larger scientific mission.

📚 Challenge Resources and Research References

The research and reference materials used in Ghosts of Space are based on the resources provided through the official NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 challenge page for:

Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars.

These resources serve as the foundation for researching historical space exploration hardware, understanding its scientific contributions, and developing accurate, engaging narratives for the game.

We use the provided materials to:

Research the history and objectives of individual space missions.
Understand the engineering and scientific instruments aboard different spacecraft.
Identify the discoveries and contributions made by these missions.
Investigate the current status and locations of historical space hardware.
Develop educational content, artifact descriptions, and interactive storytelling experiences.

All historical and scientific information incorporated into the game is intended to remain grounded in documented mission records and the resources provided for the challenge.

NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 — Abandoned but not Forgotten

Additional sources are credited wherever their information, datasets, photographs, or other materials are used.

Our Commitment to Accuracy

Ghosts of Space distinguishes documented historical facts from fictional gameplay elements. Dialogue and narrative scenes are designed to make the experience engaging, but fictional character interactions are not presented as authentic historical transmissions or NASA statements.

Mission histories, scientific discoveries, artifact locations, and operational statuses are based on available research and documented sources.



🌍 Why This Project Matters

Space exploration is often remembered through its greatest achievements: the first Moon landing, the discovery of distant worlds, and the search for life beyond Earth.

But behind every achievement are machines that made it possible.

Some completed their missions and fell silent. Others continue travelling through space. Some remain on distant surfaces, preserving the legacy of the missions that sent them there.

These machines are more than abandoned equipment. They represent decades of human curiosity, engineering, experimentation, and scientific discovery.

They may have been abandoned, but they should never be forgotten.

Through Ghosts of Space, we hope to inspire the next generation to look beyond the discoveries themselves and appreciate the machines, engineering, curiosity, and human effort that made those discoveries possible.
